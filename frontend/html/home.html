Smarter Clinic Queue Management

MEDIQUEUE helps South African clinics reduce overcrowding by allowing patients to book appointments, join virtual queues, and track their position in real time.

What is MEDIQUEUE?

MEDIQUEUE is a web-based appointment and queue management system designed for public health clinics. It improves patient flow, reduces waiting frustration, and gives real-time visibility into queue status.

What You Can Do on MEDIQUEUE

  • Appointment booking and management
  • Real-time queue tracking
  • Clinic directory with filtering by province and district
  • Staff dashboard for managing patients
  • Notifications and reminders
  • Analytics and reporting tools

User Roles

  • Patients: Book appointments, join queues and track your clinic visit in real time
  • Clinic Staff: Manage queues and update patient statuses and improve the flow of patients through the clinic
  • Admins: Manage clinics, staff profiles, services, system data and reporting

Why This System Matters

Many public clinics in South Africa face long queues, overcrowded waiting areas and uncertainty around waiting times.


MEDIQUEUE helps reduce that pressure by giving patients clearer information before and during their visit. It also supports clinic teams with better visibility of patient demand, queue movement and appointment activity.


The result is a smoother, more organised clinic experience for everyone.
